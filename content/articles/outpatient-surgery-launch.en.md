## Expanding our services for you

Dear patients,

we are delighted to announce: **our practice is expanding its range of services!** We are now pleased to offer you **outpatient minimally invasive gynecological surgery**.

Many procedures no longer require a hospital stay. Modern minimally invasive techniques allow us to operate gently, safely, and with minimal strain on the body — while you recover in the comfort of your own home.

### Which operations do we offer?

- **Hysteroscopic procedures** — diagnostic hysteroscopy, removal of endometrial polyps, endometrial resection, removal of IUDs and submucosal fibroids, division of adhesions in the uterine cavity
- **Laparoscopic procedures** — diagnostic laparoscopy, sterilization, division of pelvic adhesions, removal of ovarian cysts
- **Plastic surgery** of the external genitalia

### Safety and comfort

All operations are performed on an outpatient basis under general anesthesia in a certified surgical center, allowing patients to return home the same day. We ensure a high level of safety and comfort at every stage — from the first consultation to the follow-up visit in our practice.

### How to book

If surgery has been recommended to you, or you would like to find out whether an outpatient procedure is right for you, book a consultation by phone. We will discuss your indication and treatment options in detail and answer all your questions.

Learn more about all procedures on the [Outpatient Surgery](/en/blog/outpatient-surgery/) page.
